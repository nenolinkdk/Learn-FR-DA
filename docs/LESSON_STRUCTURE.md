# Lesson Structure

## Status

This document defines the production lesson architecture for Learn FR-DA version 1.

The core release contains:

- Level 1: 10 lessons for visitors and tourists.
- Level 2: 10 lessons for longer stays, work and everyday life.
- Enfants: 10 child-focused lessons.
- Approximately 10 dialogue/communication units per lesson.

The lesson sequence is communicative rather than grammar-driven. Grammar, pronunciation, cultural information and digital Denmark are introduced where they naturally support the situation.

## General dialogue rule

A lesson is not a list of ten unrelated phrase pairs. Each lesson is a small event unfolding in a specific place with recurring participants and a clear practical objective.

The approximately ten units may include:

- questions and answers;
- short exchanges;
- reactions;
- instructions;
- clarification;
- choices;
- practical information.

Controlled repetition is useful, but repeated generic patterns across lessons must be avoided. In particular, not every lesson should revolve around asking for help, missing information, or returning later.

French is the support language. Danish is the target language. The Danish should be contemporary, natural and useful rather than a literal rendering of French textbook sentences.

# Level 1 — Visiter le Danemark / Besøg i Danmark

Primary audience: tourists and short-term visitors.

## L1-01 — Arrivée au Danemark / Ankomst til Danmark

Situation: A traveller arrives in Copenhagen and needs to orient themselves immediately after arrival.

Communicative goals:

- recognise basic arrival information;
- ask where to go;
- understand simple directions;
- locate transport and exits;
- handle a first short interaction in Danish.

Linguistic focus:

- simple questions;
- hvor / hvad;
- present tense;
- basic politeness;
- pronunciation of common place and transport words.

Digital/cultural integration:

- signs and screens;
- journey planning on a phone;
- digital ticket expectations introduced briefly.

## L1-02 — À l'aéroport et à la gare / I lufthavnen og på stationen

Situation: The traveller finds the correct train or metro and checks departure information.

Communicative goals:

- ask about platforms and departures;
- understand times;
- ask whether a train stops somewhere;
- react to a delay or platform change.

Linguistic focus:

- numbers and clock time;
- går / kører / stopper;
- prepositions for place;
- yes/no questions and inversion.

Digital/cultural integration:

- departure boards;
- DSB/transport information;
- electronic tickets.

## L1-03 — Métro, train et bus / Metro, tog og bus

Situation: The traveller buys or activates the right ticket and completes a public-transport journey.

Communicative goals:

- ask which ticket is needed;
- understand zones/destination information;
- ask where to change;
- understand check-in/check-out instructions where relevant.

Linguistic focus:

- skal + infinitive;
- kan + infinitive;
- hvilken / hvor mange;
- imperative forms in common instructions.

Digital/cultural integration:

- Rejsekort and current travel-card concepts where relevant;
- Rejsebillet/DOT/DSB-type apps;
- mobile tickets and journey planners.

## L1-04 — À l'hôtel / På hotellet

Situation: The traveller checks in, receives practical information and resolves a small room-related issue.

Communicative goals:

- state a name/reservation;
- understand room and breakfast information;
- ask about Wi-Fi, luggage and checkout;
- report one simple problem.

Linguistic focus:

- jeg har / jeg skal;
- definite nouns;
- room numbers;
- common modal verbs.

Digital/cultural integration:

- booking confirmation on phone;
- digital room/check-in information where natural.

## L1-05 — Au café / På café

Situation: Two visitors order drinks and something small to eat at a café.

Communicative goals:

- order naturally;
- ask what something is;
- specify quantity or preference;
- understand takeaway/eat-in questions;
- pay.

Linguistic focus:

- jeg vil gerne…;
- en/et in high-frequency nouns;
- noget / en / to;
- tak and everyday Danish service register.

Digital/cultural integration:

- card/contactless payment;
- MobilePay mentioned as context, not assumed to be available to every tourist.

## L1-06 — Au restaurant / På restaurant

Situation: A small group arrives, orders a meal and asks about one dietary requirement.

Communicative goals:

- ask for a table;
- order food and drinks;
- ask about ingredients;
- understand a waiter’s short questions;
- ask for the bill.

Linguistic focus:

- plural and quantities;
- med / uden;
- hvad er…?;
- modal/polite request patterns.

Digital/cultural integration:

- QR menu where encountered;
- card/contactless payment.

## L1-07 — Magasins et paiement / Butikker og betaling

Situation: The visitor buys an everyday item and handles checkout.

Communicative goals:

- ask where an item is;
- ask price/size;
- understand bag/receipt questions;
- pay by card;
- understand a simple return/exchange explanation.

Linguistic focus:

- demonstratives;
- adjectives and simple agreement;
- numbers/prices;
- den/det.

Digital/cultural integration:

- self-checkout;
- card/contactless payment;
- electronic receipts;
- MobilePay as Danish cultural/payment context.

## L1-08 — Se repérer et visiter / Find vej og oplev byen

Situation: The traveller walks through a Danish city and asks for directions to a sight or street.

Communicative goals:

- ask where something is;
- understand left/right/straight ahead;
- ask how far it is;
- clarify a place name;
- understand a simple route description.

Linguistic focus:

- hvor ligger…?;
- der / her;
- imperatives and direction expressions;
- pronunciation of Danish place names.

Digital/cultural integration:

- maps/navigation apps;
- QR information at attractions where relevant.

## L1-09 — Billets, applications et réservations / Billetter, apps og reservationer

Situation: The visitor books or retrieves a ticket/reservation on a phone for an attraction or journey.

Communicative goals:

- ask whether booking is necessary;
- show a reservation;
- understand a QR-code or time-slot instruction;
- ask what to do when an app or ticket does not display correctly.

Linguistic focus:

- skal / kan / må;
- perfect tense in simple practical use;
- den / det referring to digital objects;
- instructions and sequence.

Digital/cultural integration:

- mobile booking;
- QR codes;
- digital tickets;
- confirmation email/SMS/app.

## L1-10 — Un problème pendant le voyage / Et problem på rejsen

Situation: Something has gone wrong: a lost item, wrong train, cancelled booking or need for practical assistance.

Communicative goals:

- explain a problem simply;
- say what happened;
- give identifying information;
- understand what the next step is;
- distinguish ordinary help from emergency situations.

Linguistic focus:

- simple perfect tense;
- fordi;
- negation with ikke;
- common past participles in fixed expressions.

Digital/cultural integration:

- finding booking/ticket information on phone;
- relevant service numbers/apps without turning the lesson into official guidance.

# Level 2 — Vivre et travailler au Danemark / Bo og arbejde i Danmark

Primary audience: newcomers, temporary residents and people moving to Denmark for work.

## L2-01 — Logement et quartier / Bolig og nabolag

Situation: A newcomer settles into a home and speaks with a neighbour or landlord about practical matters.

Communicative goals:

- describe where one lives;
- ask about laundry, waste, keys and shared facilities;
- understand simple house rules;
- arrange a practical time.

Linguistic focus:

- possessives;
- der er;
- definite forms;
- time expressions.

Digital integration:

- digital building/resident information where relevant.

## L2-02 — Courses et vie quotidienne / Indkøb og hverdag

Situation: The learner shops for groceries and handles ordinary practical choices.

Communicative goals:

- find products;
- understand offers/quantities;
- ask for alternatives;
- handle self-service checkout;
- discuss everyday needs.

Linguistic focus:

- count/non-count patterns in practical vocabulary;
- adjective forms;
- meget/mange;
- quantities.

Digital integration:

- supermarket apps/loyalty concepts where relevant;
- self-checkout and digital receipts.

## L2-03 — Premier jour au travail / Første dag på arbejde

Situation: A new employee meets colleagues and receives basic workplace information.

Communicative goals:

- introduce oneself;
- explain role/background briefly;
- ask where things are;
- understand simple workplace routines;
- participate in small talk.

Linguistic focus:

- present tense;
- job vocabulary;
- subordinate clauses introduced lightly;
- Danish informal address.

Digital integration:

- workplace login/apps mentioned only where natural.

## L2-04 — Réunions, horaires et rendez-vous / Møder, arbejdstider og aftaler

Situation: The employee coordinates schedules, meetings and deadlines.

Communicative goals:

- propose/change a time;
- say when one is available;
- understand calendar language;
- explain a minor delay;
- confirm an appointment.

Linguistic focus:

- V2 word order;
- inversion after initial adverbials;
- future expressed with present tense / skal;
- dates and times.

Digital integration:

- digital calendars and meeting invitations.

## L2-05 — Médecin et pharmacie / Læge og apotek

Situation: The learner needs non-emergency health assistance and explains basic symptoms.

Communicative goals:

- say what hurts / describe a simple symptom;
- understand appointment information;
- ask how medicine should be taken;
- show or refer to health-card information.

Linguistic focus:

- body and symptom vocabulary;
- har + noun expressions;
- gør ondt;
- modal instructions.

Digital integration:

- sundhedskort;
- digital appointment systems;
- relevant Danish health apps/services described cautiously.

## L2-06 — Commune et services publics / Kommune og offentlige tjenester

Situation: A newcomer needs to understand where to handle a practical public-service matter.

Communicative goals:

- explain what one needs to do;
- understand document requirements in simple language;
- ask where/how a process is completed;
- distinguish online and in-person steps.

Linguistic focus:

- skal / skal have;
- passive-like institutional phrases introduced carefully;
- sequence words: først, derefter, så.

Digital integration:

- borger.dk;
- CPR context;
- appointment booking.

## L2-07 — MitID et Digital Post / MitID og Digital Post

Situation: The learner encounters Danish digital identification and official digital communication.

Communicative goals:

- understand basic vocabulary around login and identification;
- ask what MitID is used for;
- understand a message about Digital Post;
- explain that login/access does not work.

Linguistic focus:

- digital verbs: logge ind, åbne, godkende, modtage;
- det virker / det virker ikke;
- perfect tense for completed actions.

Digital integration:

- MitID;
- Digital Post;
- borger.dk;
- clear distinction between language teaching and official instructions.

## L2-08 — Banque et paiements / Bank og betaling

Situation: The learner discusses ordinary payment and banking concepts relevant to living in Denmark.

Communicative goals:

- ask about payment methods;
- understand card/account vocabulary;
- discuss transfer/payment status;
- understand basic NemKonto context;
- talk about MobilePay in everyday use.

Linguistic focus:

- compound nouns;
- modal verbs;
- common transaction verbs;
- passive/result expressions in simple form.

Digital integration:

- cards/contactless;
- bank apps;
- MobilePay;
- NemKonto context.

## L2-09 — Trajet quotidien / Pendling og transport i hverdagen

Situation: The learner commutes and manages delays, connections and recurring transport choices.

Communicative goals:

- describe a regular journey;
- understand disruptions;
- ask about alternatives;
- communicate being delayed.

Linguistic focus:

- frequency adverbs;
- V2 with time expressions;
- fordi / hvis;
- present/perfect contrast.

Digital integration:

- journey planners;
- travel-card/app use;
- disruption notifications.

## L2-10 — Vie sociale et loisirs / Socialt liv og fritid

Situation: Colleagues or acquaintances arrange an informal activity.

Communicative goals:

- invite someone;
- accept/decline naturally;
- suggest an alternative;
- discuss simple interests;
- arrange time/place.

Linguistic focus:

- gerne / hellere;
- modal verbs;
- informal spoken patterns;
- subordinate clauses with fordi/hvis.

Digital integration:

- messages, bookings and event information where natural.

# Enfants — Pour les enfants / For børn

The Children module is not a simplified duplicate of the adult course. It uses child-relevant situations, short turns and concrete vocabulary. Safety situations use especially clear and unambiguous language.

## C-01 — Je m'appelle… / Jeg hedder…

Situation: Two children meet for the first time.

Goals: name, age, where one comes from, simple greeting and goodbye.

Focus: jeg/du, hedder, er, simple numbers.

## C-02 — Tu veux jouer ? / Skal vi lege?

Situation: Children meet at a playground or holiday location and start playing together.

Goals: invite, accept, decline, choose a game, take turns.

Focus: skal vi…?, vil du…?, ja/nej, min/din tur.

## C-03 — À l'école / I skolen

Situation: A child joins a class or visits a Danish school environment.

Goals: understand simple classroom instructions, ask where something is, ask to borrow something.

Focus: imperatives, hvor, må jeg…?, common school vocabulary.

## C-04 — Sport et piscine / Sport og svømmehal

Situation: Children take part in sport or visit a swimming pool.

Goals: understand simple instructions, talk about ability, find changing room/toilet, ask when an activity begins.

Focus: kan/kan ikke, skal, directions, body/action verbs.

Digital integration: entry ticket/booking where relevant.

## C-05 — Bibliothèque et café / Bibliotek og café

Situation: A child visits a library and later buys a simple snack or drink.

Goals: find books/computers, ask to use something, choose and pay for a small item.

Focus: må jeg…?, jeg vil gerne…, den/det.

Digital integration: self-service/loan screen and card/contactless payment as context.

## C-06 — Computer, internet et jeux / Computer, internet og spil

Situation: Children talk about games and using Wi-Fi/computers.

Goals: ask for Wi-Fi, talk about a game, invite someone to join, explain a simple technical problem.

Focus: spiller, bruger, virker/virker ikke, kan du…?

Digital integration: Wi-Fi, usernames/game invitations; no collection of personal credentials.

## C-07 — Magasin et vêtements / Butik og tøj

Situation: A child is with an adult buying clothes or another small item.

Goals: size, colour, price, preference, trying something on.

Focus: colours, adjectives, den/det, for stor/for lille.

## C-08 — Je me suis perdu / Jeg er blevet væk

Situation: A child cannot find the accompanying adult in a public place.

Goals: clearly say they are lost, give first name, ask for a safe adult/staff member, explain where they last saw the adult.

Focus: jeg er blevet væk, jeg kan ikke finde…, min mor/min far/den voksne.

Safety rule: language must be short, clear and non-ambiguous.

## C-09 — En transport / Med bus og tog

Situation: A child travels with family/group and needs to understand basic transport information.

Goals: identify destination, platform/stop, ask if this is the right train/bus, understand when to get off.

Focus: er det…?, hvor skal vi af?, næste stop.

Digital integration: ticket/phone journey information only in simple child-relevant form.

## C-10 — J'ai besoin d'aide / Jeg har brug for hjælp

Situation: A child needs practical or urgent help.

Goals: say they need help, describe a simple injury/problem, identify trusted staff/adult, communicate essential information.

Focus: hjælp, jeg har brug for…, det gør ondt, ring til…

Safety rule: distinguish ordinary assistance from emergency language. Emergency information must be factually checked before production release.

# Cross-course linguistic progression

The app should not force a traditional grammar syllabus, but the course should gradually expose learners to:

1. simple present and high-frequency fixed phrases;
2. question words and yes/no inversion;
3. en/et and noun definiteness;
4. modal verbs;
5. adjective forms;
6. imperative/instruction language;
7. V2 word order;
8. negation placement;
9. perfect tense in practical contexts;
10. simple subordinate clauses;
11. der/det/man;
12. common prepositions and compounds.

Pronunciation notes should prioritise difficulties that matter for intelligibility, especially Danish vowels, soft d, stød/reductions where useful, numbers, and common high-frequency forms. Pronunciation guidance should remain practical rather than becoming a phonetics course.

# Digital Denmark principle

Digital Denmark is integrated across situations rather than confined to one technology lesson. Examples include:

- transport apps and tickets;
- MobilePay and payment cards;
- self-service systems;
- health card and digital appointment systems;
- MitID and Digital Post;
- borger.dk and public-service booking;
- QR codes and electronic confirmations.

Because digital products, public systems and app names can change, production content must distinguish stable Danish-language concepts from time-sensitive service details. Time-sensitive claims should be reviewed before release updates.
